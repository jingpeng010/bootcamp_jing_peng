
# Stakeholder Context Memo

## Stakeholder
The primary stakeholder is a regulatory review organization responsible for evaluating clinical trial and drug submission materials. Its goal is to improve the efficiency, consistency, and reliability of the review process while maintaining regulatory compliance.

## End User
The primary end users are medical and regulatory reviewers who examine large volumes of clinical and regulatory documents, search for supporting evidence, and prepare structured review reports.

## Pain Points
Reviewers often need to manually search through lengthy and heterogeneous documents to identify relevant evidence. This process is repetitive, time-consuming, and may lead to inconsistent information retrieval and reporting.

## Decisions Supported
The system is intended to help reviewers determine whether submitted documents contain sufficient and relevant evidence for regulatory evaluation. It should also help reviewers identify where supporting evidence is located and which sections may require additional attention or manual verification.

## Project Goal
The project aims to build an AI-assisted medical review report system that retrieves relevant evidence from source documents and generates structured review reports. The system is designed to support, rather than replace, expert judgment.

## Success Criteria
Success will be evaluated through retrieval quality, relevance of retrieved evidence, reduction in unsupported generated content, and reduction in manual review effort. A key target is approximately a 30% reduction in review time compared with a fully manual workflow.
